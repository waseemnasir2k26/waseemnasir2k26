## 🎬 AI Video Editing — Driven by Claude + Codex

> We don't edit videos by hand. We orchestrate two AI coding agents to do it — and they batch-render at volume.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="38%" align="center" valign="top">
  <img src="./waseem-cafe-counter.jpg" width="100%" style="border-radius:16px;" alt="AI video editing pipeline" />
  <br><sub><i>One recording → a week of content.</i></sub>
</td>
<td width="62%" valign="top">

**How it works**

- **Claude Code + Codex** plan the edit, write the FFmpeg graph, and self-correct
- **Whisper** transcribes for word-accurate kinetic captions
- **pycaps + FFmpeg** burn captions, hook cards, transitions, beat-synced music
- **ElevenLabs** adds AI voiceover; sidechain-ducked BGM under it
- Multi-version, batch-rendered — reels, promos, vlogs, explainers

**What you get**

- Raw phone / drone / screen footage in → polished 9:16 + 16:9 out
- On-brand captions, color grade, and outro every time
- Turnaround in hours, not days — and at volume, not one-off

**Open-source proof:** [Reborn Forge](https://github.com/waseemnasir2k26/reborn-forge) (master-prompt generator) · [ReelForge](https://github.com/waseemnasir2k26/reelforge) (auto-caption / auto-edit) · [reel-studio](https://github.com/waseemnasir2k26/reel-studio) (5-mode reel engine).
